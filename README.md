## Private AI for your business

Imagine having your own ChatGPT-like assistant, but running on your servers or in your Azure tenant instead of someone else's cloud. That's what I build. I set up self-hosted models like Llama, Mistral and Qwen, served with **vLLM** behind an OpenAI-compatible API, so the tools you already use can plug straight in.

Once it's running, your team can simply ask questions about your internal files, wikis and email, and get answers grounded in your own documents. Since nothing is sent to third-party AI providers, it's also a much easier conversation with your GDPR and EU AI Act people. In my experience this matters most for legal, healthcare, finance and manufacturing teams, but really anyone who handles sensitive data will feel the difference.

## GPU & cloud infrastructure

Of course, a model is only as good as the platform it runs on. I help pick the right GPU servers, set them up and tune them for inference, fine-tuning or training, and I build **Kubernetes** and **Azure** platforms that keep AI workloads running smoothly. I like to keep everything as code with **Terraform**, **Ansible** and **GitOps with Argo CD**, and I watch it all with Grafana and Telegraf, so issues get spotted before your users ever notice.

## AI-powered operations

I'm also a big fan of letting AI help run the infrastructure itself. I build LLM agents and **MCP servers** that keep an eye on your systems, help troubleshoot problems and even write the documentation nobody has time for.
